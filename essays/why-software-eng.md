## Interest

<img width="350px" class="rounded float-start pe-4" src="../img/roblox-logo.png">

I, like most in my generation, grew up playing video games. I didn't have a lot of money to spend on videogames growing up, but I did have a computer at hand so anything online was fair game. This led me to playing some really bad games. The kind of game you'd open and find some kind of bug within the first couple hours by complete accident. That's where the curiosity came from, what caused these bugs and can they be reproduced and used? That's all I had mind as a kid, but that set some early interests for me. 

I have to admit, I was a little bit of <b>that</b> kid who played Roblox. It was fair given my circumstances but the kinds of games on Roblox at the time always had some kind of jank. It didn't have to be something massive like data wiping a save file, but little unintended value changes and small out-of-bounds tricks popping up every once and a while. A specific example was an glitch that allowed for phasing through thin corners due to abusing the third person to first person shift Roblox had baked into the game. This really fostered my interest in what happens behind the hood of games and everything else on the internet! 

Now I am a student who is actively studying computer science as a whole which helps explain how these systems work at a better level. It's not only videogames anymore either, there are so many different facets of computers and software that interest me. 

## The Future

<img width="350px" class="rounded float-start pe-4" src="../img/future-city.jpg">

Currently in my junior year of college, I hope to specialize more into the networks and security aspect of computers. Curiosity the main driving factor, when looking at a website or a game I want to be able to at a glance know what may be happening in the background that keeps the system running and if it is broken, see at a glance the steps needed to remedy problems. I would like to be able to work better under a time pressure. I've never really been good with deadlines and tend to take paths I would not have taken would I have had more time. There's a lot to be done to make a greater future, with the current technology landscape however, who knows what may be in store for the future.
