# Project: Study Spottah

## Overview

<strong>Problem:</strong> Finding study spots on campus outside of the library can be hard, especially if you are looking for a specific vibe or certain amenities. 
<br></br>
<strong>Solution:</strong> This application will allow UHM students to ground source a list of “hidden gem” study spots and classify them by amenities and other descriptive labels. Users will be able to view and filter the table of different study spots and they could potentially propose new study spots or leave comments about the quality of the study.

## Approach:
The main idea is to provide a list of study spots that are tagged with different descriptors to help students find new places to study on campus, which will be further known as Study Spottah. We’d have a solid amount of static data that we would have to collect and populate first for each available spot, such as:
- Location (Building, Floor, Room, Name)
- AC or not 
- Food allowed or not
- Microwave or not
- Chargers or not
- Windows or not
- Group study allowed or not
There will also be functionality to allow users to input additional notes, such as:
- Loudness levels
- Traffic, busy or not?
- Available seating?
- Quiet level [scale to 1-5]
- Upvote/downvote notes
Admins will be able to moderate such actions by doing actions such as:
- Delete malicious user inputed notes 
- Add notes
- Ban bad actor users

## Mockup Page Ideas:
Some of the following are mockup pages for the Study Spottah website are:
- Homepage displaying our mission and a list of study spots
- Login/Sign up page
- Page for each study spot with the above data points, directions and user notes
- Subsection for users to note current conditions of the spot
- Page for submitting new study spot locations to be added to the website
- Admin homepage
- Page for nearby transit current arrival times

## Use Case Ideas:
- Users without or without an account may look for locations without signing up
- Users with an account may post notes about current study spot conditions
- Users with an account may upvote/downvote notes
- Users with an account may leave reviews or feedback to study spots
- Admins may edit study spot tags, delete or upload notes and ban users

## Beyond the Basics
